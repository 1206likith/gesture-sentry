Where this leaves you

If your friend wants "better than sumo" but still low-risk: Self-balancing robot, gesture-controlled robot, or sentry turret tracker (Set 3, #1/#6/#13) — each is a genuine step up in technical depth and demo appeal without leaving the "few weeks, moderate difficulty" zone the sumo bot occupies.

If the team wants to swing for the most impressive thing on this entire page: 3-bot swarm coordination or 2-robot capture-the-flag (Set 3, #2/#14) — genuinely stand out because almost no student team attempts multi-agent coordination, but budget real extra weeks and expect more integration pain across multiple robots.

If the goal shifts back to course marks (paper/patent) instead of a standalone demo: Set 1's priority-inversion or GPIO-interrupt-storm ideas need zero extra hardware and map directly onto Module 4 (RTOS) of the syllabus.
